Paisley privacy policy

For the Paisley: Nail & Henna Studio app (package com.minormending.paisley), by Standard Mending Co., version 1.0.0 and later. Last updated: November 15, 2026.

Paisley is a nail-painting and henna-drawing app for children and families. It has no advertising, no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking. Everything a child makes stays on the device.

What stays on your device

Designs, saved pictures, the chosen skin tone and the grown-up settings are kept in the app's own storage on the device. They are never sent to us. If Android backup is on, Android may back up the app's storage to the device owner's Google account; that is controlled in Android settings.

App updates (Expo)

When the app starts with an internet connection, it asks Expo's update service (EAS Update, run by 650 Industries, Inc.) whether a newer version of the app's content is available, so fixes can arrive without a store update. That request includes the app's version and platform and a random identifier that the app creates on the device when it is installed. The identifier is not derived from the device's hardware, phone number, advertising ID or any account, and it cannot identify a person. If an update fails to start, the app may also send the failed update's ID and the error text so the faulty update can be withdrawn. Nothing a child draws or saves is ever included. Without a connection the app simply runs the version it already has. Expo's privacy policy is at expo.dev/privacy.

Notifications

If a grown-up allows it, the app schedules one reminder on the device when a henna design reaches its deepest colour. It is a local notification: no push service is used and nothing is sent anywhere. It can be turned off in the app's grown-up settings or in Android settings.

Sharing a picture

A picture leaves the device only if a grown-up answers the grown-up question and chooses to send it through the device's own share sheet. The app does not see where it goes.

What we do not collect

Paisley does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, advertising ID or hardware identifiers. It has no ads, no in-app chat and no links out of the app that a child can reach without the grown-up question.

Purchases

This version has no purchases. If a later version offers a one-time unlock, it will be made through Google Play behind the grown-up question; Google handles the payment and we never see payment details. This policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

Paisley is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families policy. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting data

Delete a design from My hands in the app, clear Paisley's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app; that removes everything it kept. The random update identifier is deleted with the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the developer email address shown under "App support" on Paisley's Google Play page.
